## Your account, back. Without the runaround.

BanOver gets locked, banned and suspended business accounts back.

1. **Paste the notice.** The diagnosis is free and tells you what happened and what you can do.
2. **Add the details.** At most 5 questions, and the documents we ask for.
3. **Approve and send.** We track every step, follow up and tell you where things stand.

Works with Meta, Google, YouTube and Amazon accounts.

We never ask for your passwords and never log in to your accounts. We never promise an outcome: the platform decides, and we keep going until it answers.

BanOver is independent and is not affiliated with any platform it works with.
